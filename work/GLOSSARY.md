# Translation glossary & style guide — 15th Five-Year Plan Outline (2026–2030)

**Source:** 《中华人民共和国国民经济和社会发展第十五个五年规划纲要》, Xinhua, 2026-03-14.
**Register:** formal Chinese government planning document. Translate in the register of an official English-language government white paper: declarative, impersonal, precise. No contractions. No "we"/"our" — the subject is the state, the Party, or the people.

## 1. Hard rules

1. **One output string per input `id`.** Never merge, split, add or drop blocks. If a source block is a heading, the output is a heading.
2. **Plain text only.** No LaTeX, no Markdown, no HTML. Use the Unicode characters “ ” for quotes, ’ for apostrophe, — (em dash), – (en dash) where needed. Do **not** escape anything.
3. **For blocks of `"type": "item"`**: the Chinese begins with `——`. Do **not** reproduce that leading dash; the typesetter adds it. Translate the sentence that follows, starting with a capital letter and ending with a full stop.
4. **Numbers, units, percentages**: keep all figures exactly. Restyle for English:
   - 140万亿元 → 140 trillion yuan; 6242万人 → 62.42 million people; 1.4万亿斤 → 1.4 trillion jin (斤 = 0.5 kg, keep "jin")
   - 58亿吨标准煤 → 5.8 billion tonnes of standard coal
   - 2.8% → 2.8%; 微克/立方米 → μg/m³ (use µg/m³ consistently)
   - PM₂.₅ → PM2.5
   - Ranges: 2026－2030 → 2026–2030 (en dash). Chinese numerals in names (第…章, 第…节) are rendered by the typesetter, so translate them normally: 第一章 → Chapter 1.
5. **Titles of documents** in 《》 → plain double quotes: 《中共中央关于制定…的建议》 → the “Recommendations of the CPC Central Committee on Formulating the 15th Five-Year Plan…”. Keep it as a title in quotes.
6. **Slogans and set phrases** — use the official English rendering given below verbatim, in quotation marks if the Chinese has them.
7. **No added commentary, no footnotes, no translator's notes.**

## 3. Sentence-level guidance
- Chinese long sentences with stacked four-character phrases: keep them as one sentence if English can carry it; otherwise split at a natural boundary, still within the same string.
- Semicolon lists (`；`) become semicolon lists (`;`).
- `坚持…、坚持…` sequences → "uphold X, Y and Z" or parallel verb clauses; keep the parallelism visible.
- Do not add "China" where the Chinese says 我国 — 我国 = "China" when a subject is needed, or "the country" when already established.
- Prefer present tense for plan commitments ("The Plan calls for…", "Effort will be made to…").
